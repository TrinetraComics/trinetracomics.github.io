WELCOME TO THE UNIVERSE

ABOUT
TRINETRA

An original Indian superhero universe built from mythology, cosmic mystery, modern India and original heroes.

A universe of connected legends.

Trinetra Comics is an original Indian superhero universe where ancient forces, modern humanity and cosmic destinies collide.

Every hero has a different origin, identity and conflict, but their stories are designed to connect. Across the comics, readers will discover forgotten powers, mysterious artifacts, extraordinary science, mythic forces and a much larger cosmic history unfolding behind individual adventures.

Our aim is to build a long-running Indian comics universe with strong characters, cinematic storytelling and stories that can move between Hindi and English while remaining part of the same continuity.
