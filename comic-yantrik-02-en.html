YANTRIK / ISSUE #2 / ENGLISH

Raktbeej

A gripping sci-fi horror superhero thriller where Yantrik must track down a terrifying mutated scientist who uses his own radioactive blood as a weapon, after the villain abducts Siddhant’s comatose mother from an impenetrable corporate fortress.

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Siddhant Singhania thought he understood the depths of his father’s corporate cruelty, but the past has returned to collect its toll in blood. When a phantom assassin violently breaches the heavily fortified Singhania Medical Wing and abducts Siddhant’s comatose mother, Maya, the rules of the game change permanently. The attacker leaves no fingerprints and uses no guns—only razor-sharp, geometric blades forged from his own floating, radioactive blood. Enter Dr. Arindam Sen, a brilliant, betrayed scientist mutated by the experimental Trinetra code into Raktbeej, an apex predator driven by psychotic vengeance against the Singhania empire. Stripped of his tactical advantages and fueled by sheer desperation, Yantrik tracks this biological nightmare to an abandoned, highly toxic bio-chem plant on the decaying outskirts of Kanpur. Trapped in a claustrophobic cage of floating blood-blades, Yantrik realizes his usual kinetic acrobatics will fail. He must rely on thermodynamics, physics, and a massive liquid nitrogen trap to freeze the unstoppable monster. However, defeating Raktbeej unearths a horrifying decade-old secret about his father’s betrayal—and places Yantrik directly in the crosshairs of a heavily armed Black-Ops mercenary squad led by his own father.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Yantrik (Real Name: Siddhant Singhania): A 23-year-old billionaire heir operating as a highly tactical vigilante. Driven by empathy and a desperate need to protect his comatose mother, Siddhant actively fights against his father’s ruthless corporate legacy. Operating completely without superpowers, he utilizes a sleek prototype suit, elite parkour agility, and his signature Mind Over Matter philosophy. In this issue, his emotional limits are pushed to the absolute brink, forcing him to suppress his panic and rely on his genius-level intellect and environmental physics to survive an overwhelmingly lethal biological threat.
ANTAGONISTS Raktbeej (Real Name: Dr. Arindam Sen): Once an idealistic scientist on the verge of a world-changing medical breakthrough (the Trinetra code), Dr. Sen was brutally betrayed, beaten, and left for dead by Vikramaditya Singhania ten years ago. Forced to inject himself with a volatile, highly toxic synthetic coagulant fused with the raw Trinetra code to survive, he mutated into a horrifying biological weapon. As Raktbeej, gravity does not affect his blood; he can manipulate his own radioactive, crimson veins, instantly crystallizing his blood into floating, razor-sharp nano-blades, buzzsaws, and massive crushing tendrils. He is a tragic but deeply psychotic villain, consumed by a decade of festering rage and a desire to tear down the Singhania empire—starting with Vikramaditya’s wife and son. Vikramaditya Singhania (Secondary/Looming Antagonist): The ruthless CEO of Singhania Corp and Siddhant’s father. While he doesn’t physically fight Yantrik in this issue, his past sins created the monster Raktbeej, and his heavily armed arrival at the climax represents the overarching, inescapable threat of the corporate empire.
