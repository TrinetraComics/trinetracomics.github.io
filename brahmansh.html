BRAHMANSH

THE IMMORTAL GUARDIAN

An ancient guardian of the Trinetra, condemned to an existence beyond ordinary time. After the Great Cosmic War, he was cast into a dark-matter comet—and eventually crashed into the Himalayas.

01 / ORIGIN

A forgotten past.
A dangerous destiny.

Brahmansh awakens on Earth with his memories fractured. He does not know why he was exiled, who sent him into the void, or why forces beyond Earth are still searching for him.

His journey begins when science, ancient mystery and cosmic danger converge at an isolated Himalayan observatory.
