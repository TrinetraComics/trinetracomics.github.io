KAVACH / ISSUE #1 / ENGLISH

The Ghost

A heavily armored, kinetic-energy-wielding operative fights a shadow war against the OMEGA syndicate while protecting his former partner and lost love, Ananya.

Posted 24 August 2026 · Published by Trinetra Comics Universe (TCU) · 43 pages · Free

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Issue #1 opens with a silent assault on an OMEGA weapons shipment before flashing back to Major Shaurya Pratap’s transformation in the Thar Desert. In the present, the Ghost Protocol keeps Shaurya legally dead as Ananya hunts the syndicate. When an ambush traps her, Kavach breaks protocol to save her, revealing just enough of Shaurya’s fighting style to ignite a dangerous hope.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Kavach (Major Shaurya Pratap): a rugged former Indian commando turned ghost operative. Ananya: Shaurya’s former partner, a lethal tactical operative hunting OMEGA.
ANTAGONISTS THE OMEGA: a clinical, silent syndicate. The Echelons: elite lieutenants with advanced bio-weapons. The Erasers: faceless strike teams with magnetic rail-carbines.
