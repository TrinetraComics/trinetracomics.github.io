⚡ THE STORM HAS ARRIVED — VAJRA ENTERS THE TRINETRA UNIVERSE

A new storm has awakened in Kashi. Atharv Shukla is only 16—a brilliant engineering prodigy whose desperate attempt to save his police officer father brings him into contact with the ancient Trinetra Fragment. The relic bonds with his neural network and awakens Vajra, the Heir of Storms. Golden and cyan lightning, tactical genius and an ancient divine power now enter the Trinetra Universe.
