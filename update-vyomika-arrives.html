TRINETRA UNIVERSE / NEW HERO

THE ARCHITECT
OF SPACE

Kolkata has a new guardian. Dr. Avni Mathur survived a catastrophic Spatial Engine explosion that fused her cellular structure with the fabric of space. Now, as Vyomika—the Architect of Space—she can fold reality, bend gravity and forge hard-light geometry. But her greatest battle is personal: her husband Kabir and five-year-old daughter Indu are trapped exactly one millimeter outside reality in The Overlap. Her first story is now part of the Trinetra Comics universe.

MEET VYOMIKA

Reality is no longer a boundary.

Vyomika joins the Trinetra roster with a power set unlike any hero before her—spatial folding, gravity manipulation, hard-light constructs and the dangerous Overlap Shift.
