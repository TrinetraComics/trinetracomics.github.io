01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Welcome to an exciting issue of the Trinetra Universe! In VAJRA: The Storm Breaks — Issue #1, gritty tactical survival violently collides with epic Indian mythology. When Inspector Saurabh Shukla’s elite police squad is ambushed and decimated by the high-tech, ruthless Pataal Syndicate in the rain-drenched ruins of Varanasi, his only hope is his 16-year-old genius son, Atharv. Armed with nothing but a canvas bag of tools, basic chemistry, and a brilliant tactical mind, Atharv must outsmart heavily armed cyborg mercenaries in a deadly game of cat-and-mouse. But intellect can only get you so far against plasma weapons. When a desperate sacrifice brings Atharv face-to-face with an ancient, divine relic known as the Trinetra Fragment, he doesn’t just survive the night—he awakens the storm. Why You’ll Love This Issue: Epic World-Building: A perfect blend of Cyberpunk-style futuristic tech and ancient Hindu mythology. High-Octane Action: Reads like a high-budget cinematic blockbuster with strategic, Die Hard-style survival tactics. A New Kind of Hero: Witness the explosive origin story of a boy who uses his brain before his brawn, until he is forced to become a god. Grab your copy of Issue #1 today and witness the explosive birth of India’s newest superhero!
