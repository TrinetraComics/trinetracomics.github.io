Vishraaj

Betrayed and murdered in a forgotten subterranean temple, Mumbai’s most lethal mercenary is resurrected by an ancient serpentine bio-weapon. He returns to the neon-lit streets not as a man, but as Vishraaj—the King of Venom—to hunt those who killed him.

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Vivan was the Mumbai underworld’s most ruthless and highly paid mercenary, a ghost who lived by the gun. His only tether to humanity was the secret orphanage he funded to save children from the brutal slums that forged him. When a high-stakes mission leads him into a hidden, occult temple deep beneath the city, Vivan is double-crossed by his employers—a shadow syndicate linked to the mysterious Kaal Mandal. Left bleeding to death on an ancient sacrificial altar, his dying blood mixes with a dormant, mystical serpentine bio-serum. He doesn't die. He evolves. Vivan awakens encased in a pitch-black, bio-organic armor, possessing superhuman agility, toxic black venom, and glowing red eyes. Issue #1: Rebirth in Venom chronicles his violent awakening, his bloody and systematic revenge against the cartel bosses who betrayed him, and the heartbreaking realization that he can never return to the human world. Shedding his past, he embraces his monstrous new form to become Vishraaj, the dark guardian of Mumbai's shadows.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Vivan / Vishraaj (The King of Venom): A former street-rat turned elite mercenary. Resurrected as a mutant anti-hero encased in bio-organic armor, he wields liquid venom and superhuman reflexes. He is a brooding, tactical genius fighting to retain his humanity while trapped in the body of a monster.
ANTAGONISTS Kaal-Dand: A ruthless Mumbai underworld boss who orchestrated Vivan’s assassination. He relies on heavy firepower and brute street thugs. The Cult of the Abyss (Kaal Mandal Initiates): Occult temple guards and dark priests who provided the sacrificial altar.
