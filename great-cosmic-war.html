01 / THE CONFLICT

A war that changed

The Great Cosmic War is one of the oldest mysteries in the Trinetra Universe. Its true causes, participants and final consequences are still being revealed.

What is known is that the war changed the lives of the guardians forever—and one of its most consequential victims was Brahmansh.
