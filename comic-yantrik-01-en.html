Grid-lock

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Corporate progress has just become a weapon. Siddhant Singhania is the heir to a ruthless billion-dollar empire, but he refuses to inherit his father’s sociopathic legacy. When Kanpur’s brand-new Smart-Grid automated transit system is violently hacked, 40-ton transport trucks are transformed into unguided missiles tearing through crowded streets. To save the city his father is trying to conquer, Siddhant must don a secretly funded prototype tactical suit and become the vigilante known as Yantrik. Armed with no superpowers—only his genius intellect, elite athletic agility, and a philosophy of Mind Over Matter—Yantrik faces impossible odds. He must confront Gridlock, a tragic cyber-terrorist strapped into a heavily modified, weaponized industrial exoskeleton. As the physical battle pushes Yantrik’s flawed prototype suit to its breaking point, he uncovers a dark, bloody secret about his own family that will change his life forever.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Yantrik (Real Name: Siddhant Singhania): A 23-year-old billionaire heir and tactical genius. Disgusted by his father’s corruption, he secretly fights for the innocent. Yantrik has no meta-human powers; he relies entirely on Olympic-level parkour, advanced physics, and specialized grappling technology to outmaneuver enemies much larger than himself. He is deeply empathetic, fiercely determined, and highly analytical.
ANTAGONISTS Gridlock (Real Name: Ramesh): The former Chief Architect of the Singhania Smart-Grid. After Vikramaditya Singhania stole his algorithm and orchestrated a deliberate traffic jam that led to the death of Ramesh’s wife, grief drove him to madness. Now, wired directly into the city’s mainframe and wearing a massive, stolen Singhania Heavy-Lift Exoskeleton, Gridlock is a walking tank seeking apocalyptic revenge against the Singhania name.
