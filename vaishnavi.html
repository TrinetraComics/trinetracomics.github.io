THE GODDESS OF COSMIC GEOMETRY

VAISHNAVI

THE GODDESS OF COSMIC GEOMETRY

Dr. Ahana Roy sought the truth of the universe through science but found it through sacrifice. Merged with an ancient Dwarka defense matrix, she arose as Vaishnavi, guardian of cosmic order, wielding the reality-splitting Rudrant.

IDENTITYSecret identity
FIRST COMICThe Forgotten Goddess
02 / POWERS & ABILITIES
POWER 01

Cosmic Geometric Manipulation

She perceives reality as interconnected geometric patterns and can manipulate those patterns to alter reality locally.

POWER 02

Energy Construct Generation

Cosmic energy becomes rigid geometric shields, platforms, chains and projectiles.

POWER 03

Dimensional Folding

Short-range teleportation by folding the geometric space between two points.

POWER 04

Flight & Cosmic Awareness

Anti-gravitational matrices enable flight, while passive awareness detects reality ripples and dimensional breaches.

ABILITY 01

Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics

Expert knowledge in quantum mechanics, geometry and ancient mathematics.

ABILITY 02

Multilingual

Fluent in Bengali, Hindi, English and Sanskrit through the Vaishnavi persona.

03 / WEAPONS & EQUIPMENT
EQUIPMENT 01

Rudrant — The Ultimate Weapon

A colossal hybrid of battle-axe and cosmic tuning fork that amplifies her geometric powers, channels cosmic fire and can split atoms.

04 / WEAKNESSES
WEAKNESS 01

Geometric Stability Dependency

Emotional or chaotic sensory overload can disrupt the calculations that hold her constructs together.

WEAKNESS 02

Mortal Host

The immortal Vaishnavi force still inhabits Ahana’s mortal body, which can suffer internal bleeding and cellular degradation under sustained output.

05 / PERSONALITY & APPEARANCE

Ahana is empathetic, scientifically curious and protective of human life. The Vaishnavi entity is ancient, detached and focused on cosmic order. Together they become an authoritative, calm warrior who fights with calculated precision rather than rage.

Athletic and graceful, with long flowing jet-black hair, liquid-gold eyes in superhero form and a dramatic black-and-crimson armored silhouette.

Costume

Advanced ballistic armor in matte black with deep crimson accents, geometric glowing power channels and a high-fashion cinched dark trench coat.

06 / CIVILIAN IDENTITY

THE WOMAN BEHIND THE GODDESS

DR. AHANA ROY

Ahana was a brilliant theoretical physicist specializing in sacred geometry and M-theory. Her search for Dwarka became the gateway to an ancient cosmic defense consciousness—and the beginning of Vaishnavi.
