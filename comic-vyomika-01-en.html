VYOMIKA / ISSUE #1 / ENGLISH

The Overlap

A brilliant architect must weaponize the laws of physics to save Kolkata from an apocalyptic terrorist plot, all while her family remains trapped exactly one millimeter outside of reality.

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

In the breathtaking premiere issue of Vyomika, we are introduced to Dr. Avni Mathur—a genius architectural engineer whose life was shattered when corporate terrorists attacked her laboratory. The resulting explosion didn’t kill her; it fused her cellular structure with the very fabric of space and gravity. She became Vyomika, the Architect of Space. But the blast ejected her husband and five-year-old daughter from our dimensional frequency, trapping them as digital phantoms in “The Overlap”—visible, but eternally out of reach.

Issue #1 kicks off with a massive, cinematic set-piece as Vyomika uses her hard-light constructs and gravity wells to save hundreds of civilians on the collapsing Howrah Bridge. But the hero’s public triumphs mask a profound, silent agony at home. When the ruthless phase-shifting terrorist Commander Vance (Rupture) returns to the rain-slicked industrial outskirts of Kolkata, Vyomika embarks on a brutal, high-octane path of vengeance. As pure spatial geometry clashes against horrific molecular decay, Vyomika discovers that VANGARD isn’t here to steal technology—they are here to tear open the sky and unleash a multidimensional apocalypse.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Vyomika (Dr. Avni Mathur): The titular hero. A stoic, genius-level intellect who commands space, gravity and hard-light geometry. She is a broken woman hiding behind a god-like power set, driven by a desperate need to solve the impossible equation that will bring her family home.

The Phantoms (Kabir & Indu): Avni’s husband and five-year-old daughter. They exist as glowing, untouchable silver and neon-purple digital static, trapped exactly one millimeter outside base reality. They are the emotional anchor of the story.
ANTAGONISTS Commander Vance (Rupture): A massive, ruthless terrorist mercenary encased in bulky cybernetic phase-armor. His touch induces instant molecular decay, turning anything—and anyone—into dead ash. He is chaotic entropy personified—the perfect foil to Vyomika’s structured mathematics.

VANGARD: A highly funded, heavily armed corporate terrorist organization seeking to manipulate multidimensional physics for global domination.
