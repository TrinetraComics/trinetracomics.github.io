Rakt-Pishach

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

The sanctuary has been breached. In a ruthless bid to draw out Mumbai's apex predator, the Kaal Mandal targets the Umeed Orphanage—Vishraaj's only remaining tether to his lost humanity. But standard weapons aren't enough to kill the King of Venom. The cyber-Aghori Warlord Kaal-Dand initiates the "Trinetra Overdrive," physically embedding a raw, unstable cosmic gem into the chest of his deadliest assassin, Rakt-Pishaach. Transformed into a walking nuclear meltdown wielding toxic-green plasma blades, the cyborg brings apocalyptic destruction to the city. Forced to abandon his shadows to protect the innocent, Vishraaj engages in a high-speed, brutal clash that stretches from the neon-lit coastal docks to the iconic suspension cables of the Bandra-Worli Sea Link. Bleeding, his pitch-black bio-armor melting from the extreme radiation, Vivan must decide how much of himself he is willing to sacrifice to stop a machine that feels no pain.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTS Vishraaj / Vivan (The King of Venom): Mumbai's lethal protector. An elite mercenary resurrected as a pitch-black, bio-armored mutant, driven by guilt and a fierce protective instinct for the orphans he left behind.
ANTAGONISTS Upgraded Rakt-Pishaach: A terrifying Kaal Mandal cyborg assassin powered by a raw Trinetra gem embedded in his chest. Kaal-Dand: The warlord of the Kaal Mandal, a cold, calculating architect of nightmares who views mass destruction and the loss of soldiers as combat data.
