Kankaali

As the legend of Mumbai’s new apex predator grows, the mysterious Kaal Mandal syndicate dispatches "Kankaali"—a terrifying, bone-manipulating assassin—to hunt down the King of Venom. To protect the slums of Dharavi, Vishraaj must push his new bio-organic mutation to its absolute limits.

01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

Weeks have passed since Vivan's violent resurrection. The Mumbai underworld is in a state of sheer panic. The cartels are being systematically dismantled in the dark by a creature the streets are calling "Vishraaj." However, this disruption of the criminal ecosystem attracts the attention of Kaal-Dand, the cyber-Aghori warlord of the Kaal Mandal. Intrigued by reports of a venomous, unkillable mutant, Kaal-Dand sends his most sadistic tracker to test the King of Venom: Kankaali. Kankaali is a horrifying fusion of dark occult rituals and cybernetics, capable of growing, shaping, and weaponizing her own calcified skeletal structure. The hunt begins in the claustrophobic, rain-drenched alleys of the Dharavi slums, threatening the very people Vivan swore to protect. Pushed into a brutal, visceral game of cat-and-mouse through an abandoned, labyrinthine meat-packing plant, Vishraaj discovers that his impenetrable black bio-armor can be pierced. Issue #2: Kankaali forces Vivan to master the tactical use of his Venom-Whips and rapid healing factor, transforming him from a resurrected victim into a strategic, lethal superhero.

02 / STORY PROFILE
PROTAGONISTSVishraaj (The King of Venom). Still grappling with his lost humanity, Vivan is learning the true extent—and the agonizing limitations—of his pitch-black bio-armor and liquid venom. He is fiercely protective of Dharavi, operating as a lethal ghost to keep the criminal element at bay.
ANTAGONISTSKankaali (The Bone-Weaver): An elite Kaal Mandal assassin. Kaal-Dand (In the Shadows): The warlord of the Kaal Mandal, observing the battle through Kankaali’s cyber-optics and gathering combat data.
