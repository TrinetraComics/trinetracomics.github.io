01 / ISSUE DESCRIPTION

A Fallen God. A Deadly Blizzard. The Universe's Greatest War Has Reached Earth.

When a dark-matter comet violently crashes into the desolate, freezing peaks of the Himalayas, Dr. Tara, a brilliant astrophysicist, braves a deadly Category-5 blizzard to investigate. Instead of a meteorite, she finds a man... an amnesiac, hyper-muscular fallen god radiating unimaginable cosmic power. Stripped of his memories, he is the last surviving Guardian of the Trinetra. He is BRAHMANSH.

But Earth is no safe haven. Hunted by the heavily armed Kanchenjunga Syndicate and a terrifying robotic pursuit drone, a powerless Brahmansh and Dr. Tara must rely on human grit and intellect to survive the freezing night. As Brahmansh's stunning, cosmic First Shield Armor finally awakens to repel the mercenaries, a much darker, ancient evil—the gargantuan warlord Malacor—stirs in the deep cosmos. The universe's greatest war has just found a new battlefield.

Step into the expansive Trinetra Comics Universe! Brahmansh Issue #1: The Celestial Descent is a breathtaking, high-octane cinematic graphic novel blending gritty Himalayan survival with explosive, vibrant cosmic superhero action.

Perfect for fans of: Epic world-building, invincible superheroes, Indian comic books, sci-fi fantasy, and cosmic mythology.

Grab your copy now and witness the birth of Earth's ultimate cosmic shield!
